## Summary (long description)

The fleet currently holds **88,211 PDF pages** across 13 sites — roughly **5.9× the 15,000-page ceiling** that was previously estimated. The distribution is heavily skewed: archive-prod alone accounts for **58% of all pages** (51,010 pages across 1,200 PDFs averaging 42.5 pages each). Only two sites — archive-prod and icjia-agency-prod — individually exceed the 15,000 estimate.

This is an **as-is snapshot**, not a final remediation target. After culling agendas, old meeting minutes, and superseded R&A publications, the remediable total is expected to drop substantially — **rough guess: ~55,000–65,000 pages, pending triage**.

The figure also excludes 421 DOCX, 17 PPTX, and 777 XLSX files (no page counts extracted yet); including rough estimates for those would push the as-is total to ~92,000+.

## As-is snapshot — what this counts

These are the pages that **exist in the fleet right now**. The number reflects the current state of files on the production sites at the time of the May 22, 2026 audit. It does **not** reflect the remediation target.

After a triage pass that removes:

- expired or routine **agendas**,
- superseded **meeting minutes**,
- **old / withdrawn R&A publications** (Research & Analysis),
- duplicate or obsolete archive material,

…the remediable population should drop meaningfully. A rough planning estimate is **~55,000–65,000 pages**, but the actual figure depends on the triage criteria and will need to be confirmed by the content owners.

---

## Caveats

1. **PDFs only.** The 88,211 figure excludes 421 DOCX, 17 PPTX, and 777 XLSX files. Rough page-equivalent estimates (~7 pages/DOCX, ~20 slides/PPTX, 1 per XLSX) would push the true as-is total to roughly **92,000+ pages**.
2. **99.7% PDF coverage.** 9 of 3,537 PDFs failed introspection (encrypted or corrupt). Their page counts are not in the 88,211 figure.
3. **Pages = volume, not work effort.** A PDF that already has a proper text layer and tag tree (`introspection.hasTags` + `textLayerCoverage` in the audit data) needs less remediation than an image-only or scanned PDF. Volume is a ceiling; effective workload is lower.
4. **Skewed distribution.** archive-prod alone accounts for 58% of all pages (51,010 across 1,200 reports averaging 42.5 pages each), so the cull-vs-keep decisions on that one site dominate the final remediation total.

## Methodology

Page counts come from `pdfjs-dist`, the same engine used by every other audit step. Per-PDF page counts are recorded as `entry.introspection.pageCount` in the inventory NDJSON (`src/introspect/pdf.js`, `src/schema/inventory.js`). The fleet rollup ships these in `audit-fleet.ndjson`. The chart is generated by `assets/fleet-pdf-pages-chart.py` and rasterized with `rsvg-convert -w 1520`.
